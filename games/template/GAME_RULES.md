# Take-away (template game)
- 2 players alternate; a pile starts with 21 stones.
- A move removes 1, 2 or 3 stones (move id = stones-1). Player 0 moves first.
- The player who takes the last stone wins. No draws.
- Optimal play: leave a multiple of 4 stones (first player wins from 21 by taking 1).
